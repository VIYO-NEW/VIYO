Per the deliverable's declared structure (reconciliation audit reporting verifiable observations), descriptor labels must match the arithmetic they introduce.
- REQUIRED CHANGE: Replace the parenthetical "(23 distinct page IDs, but two title-IDs are duplicated at the page-ID level — see §1.4 Anomaly 1)" with accurate descriptor — e.g., "(23 distinct nominal Decision IDs across the format; D67 + D68 each have 2 page IDs at the page-ID level per §1.4 Anomaly 1, yielding 25 physical page IDs total)" or equivalent.
