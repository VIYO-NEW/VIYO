### Finding 5 — VALID

### Finding 9 — VALID
- REASONING: §4's first communication bullet states "Lead with RULE A 1-paragraph summary" as an unconditional instruction. ### Finding 10 — VALID
- REASONING: §3 Role Context declares the Decision Cataloger role "formal role active as of CLAUDE.md v2 commit moment." §5 Top 1 transitional clause simultaneously acknowledges that "Cataloger role tool-access scoping" is not yet operationalized and authorizes Architect to perform canonical writes during the transitional period. ANTI_PATTERN_CATALOG.md §3 Category 3 names the operational substrate for the Cataloger role distinction: "Role-scoped tool access | MCP / tool runtime configuration | Architect role lacks Notion write tools; Curator role lacks Notion write tools; only Cataloger role has Notion DB write tools enabled | Tool invocation time (the write tool literally isn't available to non-Cataloger roles)." Until tool-access scoping is operational, the Cataloger role has no enforcement mechanism distinguishing it from Architect — declaring the role "formal" without its enforcement substrate re-creates the dependency on agent discipline that the catalog §3 Counter-Pattern explicitly says topology separation is supposed to replace ("not by trust or self-discipline"). The internal contradiction is real: the file declares the role formal in §3 and acknowledges its enforcement substrate is pending in §5.
- REQUIRED CHANGE: Reconcile §3 and §5 Top 1. Two paths: (a) downgrade §3's claim about Cataloger formality to reflect that the role is defined but not yet operationally enforced (e.g., describe the role per its target operational substrate, and surface the tool-access scoping gap as an explicit pending operational item) and remove or restructure §5 Top 1's transitional clause per Finding 3; or (b) defer declaring the role "formal" in CLAUDE.md until tool-access scoping is operational and surface that operational gap as a Curator-call to PO for resolution before re-asserting the role declaration. The fix path that closes Finding 3 also closes part of Finding 10; the Author may treat them as coupled when applying.
